https://www.teepublic.com/poster-and-art/541555-mlp-cutie-mark-rainbow-special-princess-celestia

All upstream artwork remains subject to its original rights and licensing terms. This file documents provenance and attribution only.

## Pinkie Pie and Sweetie Bot

The copy-action artwork (`src/assets/pinkie-mirror.png`) depicts Pinkie Pie looking at her reflection, with a transparent background. It is a generated adaptation of the scene shown at 9:57 in this video:

https://youtu.be/t8PNXM7MoQM?t=597

The ChatGPT button artwork (`src/assets/sweetie-bot.png`) depicts Sweetie Bot, a robot interpretation of Sweetie Belle. Both button images were created using OpenAI image generation. The linked video and original show artwork remain subject to their respective rights; this attribution does not grant or imply a license.
